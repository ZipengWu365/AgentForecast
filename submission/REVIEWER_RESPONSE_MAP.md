# Reviewer Response Map

This map groups anticipated criticism into `accept`, `reframe`, and `defer`.

## Accept

- community adoption is still limited
- optional adapters are not all reviewed
- internal benchmark outputs are not external performance evidence
- cross-domain demos are not domain-validation studies

## Reframe

- contribution type: software-first scientific software, not new-method novelty
- reviewed boundary: compact pack, strict research API, artifact contract, tool/MCP surface
- streaming pilot: research annex, not headline claim

## Defer

- learned backend selection
- unified uncertainty contract across more backends
- stronger streaming research program with broader evidence
